06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
to know.

Do you film the videos in my country?+

The international packages are delivered remotely. We provide scripts and shot guides; you or a local videographer capture the footage. On-site crews are a separate quote, subject to availability.

Are videos unique to each account?+

Package quantities count original edited videos. We adapt and cross-post each approved video to your managed accounts where appropriate. Eight originals shared to two accounts can create sixteen placements; they remain eight originals.

What happens to comments and messages?+

Managed Marketing and Marketing & Lead Support include handling during agreed coverage hours, within the package allowance. We use approved answers, ask agreed qualification questions and pass relevant enquiries to your team. Urgent issues and sensitive questions are escalated.

Do you guarantee leads or property sales?+

No. We commit to the agreed content and handling service. Reach, enquiries and sales depend on the market, offer, footage, advertising and your team’s follow-up. Conversation limits are workload allowances, not guaranteed lead volumes.

Are advertising and translations included?+

Paid advertising spend and campaign management are separate. Packages cover one agreed content language. We confirm language support, translations and local market requirements before onboarding.

How do we start, and what are the terms?+

We first confirm your goals, accounts, filming capacity, content language, coverage hours and handoff process. You receive a written scope covering onboarding, payment, approvals, timing and cancellation before work begins.
