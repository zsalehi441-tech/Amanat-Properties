BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful answers.

Can I list a property here?

This website introduces Amanat and our partnerships. Approved partners submit properties through Amanat System. Customers can browse published listings at amanat.af.

How do I get a partner account?

Contact us first. After agreeing the partnership, HQ creates your office account and assigns its city access.

Can other partners see my clients?

Partner access is limited to their own office’s records. HQ access is assigned according to each staff member’s role and responsibilities.

What are the partnership terms and fees?

We discuss the applicable services and partnership terms before work begins. Speak with HQ about the details for your business.
